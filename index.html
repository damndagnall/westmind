Meet Taine Hall

Taine brings a deeply compassionate and curious approach to therapy, grounded in the belief that meaningful change emerges from a secure, trusting therapeutic relationship. With experience across community Alcohol and Other Drug services and private hospital settings, he works with individuals navigating a wide spectrum of mental health challenges.

Drawing from psychodynamic and attachment theories, Taine helps clients explore the often unconscious patterns that shape their emotional and relational experiences. By bringing these patterns into awareness, new possibilities emerge—for understanding yourself differently, relating to others more authentically, and engaging with the world in more fulfilling ways.

While his work is rooted in depth psychology, Taine integrates evidence-based modalities including CBT, DBT, Schema Therapy, Narrative Therapy, and Mindfulness practices, creating a tailored approach that honors your unique journey and needs.

Taine also has experience working with victims of motor vehicle accidents receiving care under the Insurance Commission of Western Australia (ICWA). If you have been injured in a road accident and are experiencing psychological distress as a result, Westmind Psychology can support your recovery — please get in touch to discuss your situation and confirm your coverage.

Helpful Information

Practical details for starting therapy with Westmind Psychology

Does Westmind Psychology accept Medicare?

Yes. With a Mental Health Treatment Plan from your GP, eligible clients can claim a Medicare rebate of $101.55 for item 80110 sessions. Up to 10 rebated individual sessions are available per calendar year.

Does Westmind Psychology offer telehealth sessions?

Yes. Secure video therapy sessions are available for clients throughout Western Australia, providing the same thoughtful therapeutic space from the comfort of your home.

What therapy approaches does Taine Hall use?

Taine integrates psychodynamic and attachment-based therapy with evidence-based modalities including CBT, DBT, Schema Therapy, Narrative Therapy, and Mindfulness practices, tailored to each client's needs.

Crisis Support

Westmind Psychology is not a crisis service. If you are in immediate danger, call 000. For 24/7 crisis support in Australia, call Lifeline on 13 11 14.

Fees & Cancellations

Session fees, Medicare eligibility, private health options, and cancellation terms can be confirmed before your first appointment or during the booking process.

Privacy & Confidentiality

Your personal information is handled confidentially and used only for appropriate clinical and administrative purposes. Limits to confidentiality can be discussed before therapy begins.
